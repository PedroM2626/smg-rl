# What prior experiments established and what can be reused

This research register covers the principal completed interventions rather than
listing every individual training block, seed, crash, fixture or benchmark.
Detailed provenance and sub-experiments are in the linked reports and the
[experiment history](experiment-history.md). None of these agent studies has
demonstrated a native RAM-confirmed full-start star. Failed screens do not prove
that an entire algorithm family or hypothesis cannot work.

| Intervention | Observed result and limitation | Reuse or worthwhile follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Visual novelty and intrinsic reward](exploration-failure-analysis.md) | Screen changes and moving platforms could be mistaken for novelty; later model-free runs without novelty still spun or idled. A single cause was not established. | Keep the diagnosis and stationary fixtures. If retesting curiosity, compare matched no-curiosity and candidate rewards using real progression and avoid visual-motion reward farming. |
| [World-model ablations](world-model-ablation.md) and [SMG2 online comparison](staged-training.md#galaxy-2-online-comparison-model-benefit-without-navigation-benefit) | Some terminal-prediction metrics improved and allowed more imagined updates, but no star or convincing navigation benefit followed. Existing evaluation reused data, and changed prediction is not a trained-policy success. | Retain checkpoints, gate code and diagnostic data. Revisit only if real-world learning and time-to-success improve under matched budgets; do not count imagined transitions as real steps. |
| [SMG1 boundary repair](smg1-boundary-repair.md) and [motor8 pilot](motor8-and-algorithms.md#first-offline-fit-review-october-25) | Prediction repairs improved some errors but early terminal gates continued to fail, and initial actors could not be treated as learned controllers. | Reuse detector and gated-learning infrastructure; do not relax original quality thresholds. A fresh unseen validation set is needed for any model claim. |
| [Stronger SMG1 curiosity](staged-training.md#final-galaxy-1-result-and-staged-experiment-completion) | Matched 10,000-decision branches yielded zero stars and no clear improvement; one-seed outcomes and terminal calibration were mixed. | Reuse matched replay audits. If revisited, test after resolving reward/control and use independent training runs; historical held-out draws are not new game states. |
| [Eight-action bootstrap](motor8-and-algorithms.md#reviewed-bootstrap-completion-october-25) | No mission was solved, and the 4,096-decision initial SMG1 actor had zero model/actor updates. | Reuse action-signature and accounting validation, not its prior-driven trajectories as learned success. |
| [Frame-skip and direction carry](regional-intervention.md#separate-temporal-control-comparison) | Frozen weak policies at four frames navigated less; twelve-frame direction carry showed a small SMG2 signal. No stars. The four-frame model was not trained for that rate. | The precise input-equivalence fixture is reusable. A newly trained 4-frame policy or a matched carry-jump trial remains an untested hypothesis. |
| [Regional rewards and curated starts](regional-intervention.md) and [six-worker run](regional-six-training.md) | Validated prefixes and safely audited starting states enabled collection but did not establish learned full-course completion. Some conveyor states drift. | Keep contact, HP and state-drift checks. Intermediate states may support exploration, but test them independently and evaluate final policies from the canonical start. |
| [Rainbow versus IQN](model-free.md#final-iqn-result-and-comparison-closure) | Matched small pilots gave zero stars and frozen spin/idle policies; IQN had lower measured update overhead here. One training seed does not establish general algorithm superiority. | Reuse instrumentation and fixed evaluations. Only repeat after objective and observation issues are addressed, with matched budgets and separate replays. |
| [IMPALA and simple objective](simple-reward-impala.md) and [frozen IQN](frozen-iqn-evaluation-20261027.md) | Removing delayed-failure preference and using a trainable visual encoder improved infrastructure; frozen policies still did not complete missions. The documented four-hour continuation later stopped early on disk exhaustion. | Preserve validated checkpoints, pixel stacks and failure fixes. A longer controlled continuation is meaningful, but do not count unsaved failed-block tail as a checkpoint. |
| [Frozen-feature genetic search](genetic-hour.md), [overnight continuation](genetic-overnight.md) and [XYZ-distance GA](genetic-goal-distance-20261027.md) | The earlier head-only GA reached an 8.0665% grounded start-goal projection before dying. Later distance-fitness search did not improve its imported champion and had zero stars. These are geometric proxies. | Retain the champion, genome layout and six-worker evaluator. Reevaluate robustness and actual full-start stars before another search; its visual encoder was inherited and frozen. |
| [Full-visual genetic search](genetic-visual.md) | Mutated vision did not exceed the unchanged imported champion during the bounded trial; it was not a perfectly isolated visual-learning ablation. | Avoid drawing a general conclusion that a trainable encoder is useless. A future search needs a better matched mutation scheme, budget and source comparison. |
| [Stochastic GA robustness and mutation calibration](robust-genetic-plan-20261027.md) | The deterministic champion was fragile to action noise; none of three mutation scales passed the preset viability gate. The proposed larger-population arms were not run. | Reuse real per-candidate traces and stochastic evaluation. A new mutation operator or alternative source may be calibrated as a new experiment; do not report unrun arms as failed. |
| [Expanding-route IQN](dynamic-route-night.md) | 100,884 real decisions, no star, and no accepted route extension; the opening reward was also weakened. | The owner's later complete human routes supply previously missing coverage. A new test should preserve a meaningful reward scale and separately validate shortcuts/landing. |
| [Reward-scale short A/B](reward-scale-study-20261028.md#completed-matched-comparison) | Four short matched blocks, zero stars and an inconsistent candidate. It failed its predeclared gate. | Preserve weights, videos and logs; this does not rule out every scale. A new adequately budgeted comparison with distinct signatures may test a better-specified objective. |
| [Partial scripted demonstrations](chocolate-frontier-search-20261028.md#completed-demonstration-comparison) | Short partial demonstrations did not pass the two-seed continuation gate; none of the 16 frozen episodes collected a star. | Reuse the physical full-start clips and offline infrastructure, but not as complete successes. The new human full-level demonstration is much more informative; action/timing incompatibility needs explicit handling. |
| [Scripted Sweet Sweet exploration](sweet-manual-search-20261028.md) | Physical scripted actions reached a partial grounded location without a star. | Keep the trajectory as a diagnostic fixture; it does not demonstrate a learned policy. The complete human recording supersedes it as course-coverage evidence. |
| [Human-route short A/B](human-route-comparison-20261028.md#completed-comparison) | All 16 frozen trials scored zero under the strict route metric, with zero stars; the two rewards could not be distinguished by that coarse metric. | Reuse verified source checkpoints and videos. The separate [one-hour-per-arm continuation](reward-hour-pair-20261028.md) used a first-segment diagnostic later withdrawn as invalid; it did not change either training reward. See the [correction](progress-metric-interpretation-20261028.md). |

Retained ROMs, savestates, profiles, replays and checkpoints are local and must
not be committed. Human recordings verified by full-start RAM and exact replay
map the courses, but do not show that an agent learned them. Restoring the same
Wii state under several action seeds does not create independent environments.
